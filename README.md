# Family Wealth Operating System (FWOS)

> **Version:** 3.0 (Foundation)
> **Owner:** Kapil Dev Yadav
> **Started:** July 2026

---

# Overview

Family Wealth Operating System (FWOS) is a long-term, goal-based wealth management framework designed to manage every aspect of a family's financial life with the discipline of a professional Family Office.

Rather than focusing on individual investments, FWOS provides a structured decision-making system that aligns every financial decision with predefined life goals, risk tolerance, and long-term wealth creation principles.

FWOS is intended to remain relevant for the next 20+ years while evolving through controlled versioning.

---

# Mission

Build a resilient family balance sheet that enables financial independence, fully funds children's education, supports entrepreneurship, protects against financial risks, and creates generational wealth through disciplined investing.

---

# Vision

To manage family wealth with the discipline of an institutional investment office by combining:

* Evidence-based investing
* Goal-based financial planning
* Passive-first portfolio construction
* Tax-efficient implementation
* AI-assisted research with human validation
* Continuous annual review and improvement

---

# Objectives

FWOS aims to:

* Define a long-term investment philosophy.
* Document family financial goals.
* Maintain a complete financial profile.
* Establish strategic asset allocation.
* Provide clear investment governance.
* Reduce behavioural mistakes.
* Standardize annual financial reviews.
* Enable AI-assisted financial decision-making.
* Preserve financial knowledge across generations.

---

# Guiding Principles

1. Family goals come before investment returns.
2. Asset allocation is more important than product selection.
3. Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity.
4. Long-term discipline beats short-term forecasting.
5. Behaviour determines investment success.
6. Every investment must have a defined purpose.
7. Costs and taxes matter.
8. Diversification reduces uncompensated risk.
9. Financial protection precedes wealth creation.
10. Continuous learning improves long-term outcomes.

# Versioning Policy

Semantic Versioning will be used.

* Major Version — Significant architectural or strategic changes.
* Minor Version — New capabilities or policies.
* Patch Version — Corrections and documentation updates.

Example:

* v3.0.0
* v3.1.0
* v3.2.0
* v4.0.0
